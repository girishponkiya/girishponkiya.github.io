Open Core Healing

Add to Home Screen · iPhone (Safari)

  1. Tap the Share button (square with arrow).
  2. Choose Add to Home Screen.
  3. Keep the name Core Healing and tap Add.

Add to Home Screen · Android (Chrome)

  1. Tap the ⋮ menu (top right).
  2. Choose Add to Home screen (or Install app).
  3. Tap Add.

The icon opens the app directly in your browser, using the Google account you are already signed in with (the one your clinic added).
